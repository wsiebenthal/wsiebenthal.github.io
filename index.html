About

Wesley Siebenthal, Principal R&D Engineer in medical devices and mechatronics. He has 20 years of mechanical engineering experience, including 10 years in medical devices, with work at Intuitive Surgical and NASA Ames. Today he develops implantable drug-delivery hardware as an independent R&D engineer, and he also designs small consumer products such as straps, mounts, gadgets and accessories. He works remotely from Daytona Beach, FL.
